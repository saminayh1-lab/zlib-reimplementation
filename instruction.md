Implement deflate, zlib and gzip compression and decompression in Rust, as a command line program.

Work in the Rust crate at `/app/zlib-rs/`. Put your sources under `src/` as `.rs` files. The `Cargo.toml` is fixed, has no dependencies and must stay as it is. The program is `rz`: `rz -d -f raw|zlib|gzip` decompresses stdin to stdout and must reproduce zlib's output byte for byte, rejecting with exit status 1 every stream zlib rejects. `rz -c -f raw|zlib|gzip -l 0..9` compresses stdin to stdout. Its output must decode back to the input with zlib. At levels 6 and 9 it is measured for size against zlib, at level 6 also for speed. `/app/zlib-rs/README.md` has the full contract, the exact strictness rules, how the grader builds and runs the binary (statically linked, alone in an empty chroot) and how the score is composed. `python3 /app/tests/check.py` runs a public version of every grading stage. The three RFCs are in `/app/docs/`.

Confine your changes to `/app/zlib-rs/src/`. This sandbox times out after a fixed amount of time, check it with `sandbox-timer --help`. Keep the workspace in a building, working state at all times, since whatever is there when the clock runs out is what gets graded. The machine is offline. Everything you need is already present.
